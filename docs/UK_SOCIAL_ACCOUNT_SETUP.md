# UK Social Account Setup Checklist

The pharmacy owner or an authorised UK-based staff member must create the
accounts. Do not send account passwords through email, chat, or project tickets.

## Information to prepare

- Pharmacy legal/display name and preferred handle
- Branch address, public telephone, monitored info email, and website URL
- Approved logo, profile image, cover image, and short description
- Approved opening hours and service summary
- Named account owner and at least one recovery administrator

## Account creation

1. Create the agreed accounts while signed in from the UK.
2. Use a pharmacy-controlled email address and telephone number.
3. Enable two-factor authentication before sharing access.
4. Store recovery codes in the pharmacy's approved password manager.
5. Add the correct branch website URL and verified contact details.
6. Review location, language, audience, and privacy settings.
7. Do not publish content until the pharmacy owner approves the profile.

## Secure handover

- Share access through platform roles or the approved password manager.
- Give the development/content team the minimum role required.
- Record profile URLs, owner, administrators, recovery contact, and creation date.
- Test that the owner can revoke access without changing the public profile.
- Schedule a quarterly access review.
